Brand system

The identity is built on one idea: a prism. A single beam of light goes in and leaves as a spectrum. For Ingenious Concepts, the beam is the client's brief and the spectrum is the five practices that deliver it. Every practice owns one color, in the same order, everywhere it appears.

Logo

Construction: an equilateral prism with a soft Ion tint, one white beam entering the left face and five rays leaving the right face. Keep clear space equal to the prism's height on every side, and never recolor or reorder the rays. The wordmark is Unbounded SemiBold, set on two lines with tight leading.

Spectrum colors only ever mean a practice, and they're used for marks (dots, borders, rays), never for body text. The light-theme values are deepened so every mark keeps at least 3:1 contrast on Lumen, the WCAG minimum for non-text elements.

Dark and light

Both themes share the same token names, so every component switches with one attribute. Dark is the default, because the prism and its rays read best as light against Prism Ink.
