How a project runs

The same four stages whether we're shipping an app, a campaign or an expo stand.

  1. 1

    Scope

    A call to agree the goal, the budget and the number we'll be judged on. You get a written plan within three working days.

  2. 2

    Prototype

    Something real to react to in two to three weeks: a working prototype, a test campaign or an event concept.

  3. 3

    Build and launch

    One project lead coordinates every practice involved, with a weekly demo and a shared board you can check anytime.

  4. 4

    Measure and scale

    We report against the number agreed in week one and move budget toward what's working.
